Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026

RoadCopy collects no data. The app has no account, uses no internet, no microphone, no ads and no analytics.

Your progress and “My details” (city, cargo, company, name) are stored on your phone. The app sends them to no one. If you have Apple or Google device backup turned on, they are included in that backup, which is yours. Deleting the app deletes them from the phone.

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your name or payment details.

This website uses no cookies and no trackers.
